# Permission from the author of KaleidoPaint

KaleidoPaint is the work of Jeff Weeks (https://www.geometrygames.org/KaleidoPaint/).
Its source is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 2
or later, which is what allows this port. The licence does not cover the
name or the icon, so Cinderblock Software asked.

On 2026-11-10 Jeff Weeks agreed to:

- an Android port released as free software under the GPL;
- the name "KaleidoPaint for Android";
- the use of his KaleidoPaint icon;
- a link in the app inviting donations to Gathering 4 Gardner
  (https://www.gathering4gardner.org/donate/).

The correspondence itself is kept by Cinderblock Software and is not part of
this repository.
